Health

Where scarcity shows up in bodies rather than balance sheets.

Spending vs outcomes · 27 nations · OECD data

What Health Care Buys

The United States spends $13,473 per person and has the worst infant mortality and shortest life expectancy of any rich country. Japan spends $5,365 and leads the world on both. Twenty-seven systems compared, each with a synopsis and rated wait times for physicals, minor surgery, and major surgery.

Disease Hotspot Map

Commodities

The things that get grown, dug up, and priced.

Annotated chart · BLS APU0000703112 · 2008–2026

Ground Beef Price, 2008–2026

Eighteen years of retail ground beef prices annotated by scarcity type: the real drought-driven herd shortage of 2011–2015, the COVID meatpacking bottleneck of 2020, and the compound tariff-and-screwworm stack driving prices above $6.80 in 2026. A companion to the dispatch on which shortage is actually behind the beef price.

Interactive map · USDA NASS data

Crop Production Map

Select any commodity, corn, soybeans, wheat, cotton, hay, and more, and a year from 2000 to 2023. The map shades each state by total output, with a ranked sidebar and per-state tooltip showing volume and share of national production. Data from USDA NASS Quick Stats; requires a free API key.

Energy

What it costs, how long it takes, and where it comes from.

Generation mix · 37 countries · 2025

How the World Makes Electricity

France runs on nuclear, Norway on water, South Africa on coal, Iran almost entirely on gas. Every country’s generation mix broken into coal, gas, oil, nuclear, hydro, wind, solar, and biomass, sortable by any source. In 2025 renewables passed coal worldwide for the first time in a century.

Open the chart ›

Cost comparison · 19 countries · three measures

What a Barrel Actually Costs

Saudi Arabia lifts a barrel of oil for about three dollars and needs roughly eighty-six to balance its budget. That spread is not geology, it is politics. Extraction cost by country, split into what it costs to pump, what it costs to develop, and what it costs to govern on.

Open the comparison ›

Cycle-time chart · land · shallow · deepwater

From Seismic Survey to Gas Pump

Every phase between a geologist's first survey and a gallon in your tank, for land, shallow water, and deepwater drilling. A land well takes about sixteen months. A deepwater project takes nearly nine years. And seven in ten high-impact exploration wells find nothing worth producing at all.

Dual-axis chart · EIA · Baker Hughes · 2015–2025

Rig Count & Brent Crude

Ten years of US rotary rig count and Brent crude spot price on one chart. The lag between price and drilling tells the story of shale economics and OPEC+ strategy, visible at every inflection from the 2015 price war through the COVID crash and the Ukraine shock.

Oil Production Map

Select any year from 1981 to 2025 to see how US crude oil production is distributed across states. Data from the EIA. Texas produces nearly half the national total. New Mexico and North Dakota have surged since the shale boom. Alaska's share has fallen every decade since its 1988 peak.

Politics

Who decides, who pays, and where that has moved over time.

Spending vs attainment · 32 countries · World Bank · PISA

What Education Spending Buys

Ireland devotes 2.9 percent of GDP to education, the least of any rich country here, and finishes near the top on both degrees and reading. Iceland devotes 7.3 percent and finishes below average. Across all 32 countries the correlation between spending and outcomes is almost exactly zero.

Enrolment and stay rates · IIE · NSF · 1960–2026

Who Came, and Who Stayed

International enrolment went from 48,486 in 1960 to 1.18 million in 2025, and roughly three quarters of international STEM doctorates were still living in the country years after graduating. Nearly every inflection in the line sits beside a change in the law rather than the economy.

Admissions and fiscal ledger · DHS · National Academies

What an Immigrant Costs

Green cards issued each year since 1960, against per-capita federal taxes paid and benefits received. Native-born citizens consume more federal resources per head than first-generation immigrants under every scenario the National Academies modelled, and the whole argument turns on one accounting assumption worth $3,613 a head.

Interactive chart · BLS · BEA · Treasury · 1940–2026

The Economy Under Each Party

Unemployment, real GDP growth, inflation, and the national debt, drawn over a band chart of which party held the White House. Toggle each series, hover any year, and judge the record for yourself. The one clean pattern is the debt ratchet, and it belongs to both parties.

Federal Donor & Consumer States

Which states pay more in federal taxes than they receive in federal spending, and does it follow party lines? Select any fiscal year from 2008 to 2023 to see grants, direct payments, and contracts by state, colored by political lean.

The Political Spectrum Gauge

Where does each country sit on the spectrum from communism to fascism, and how did it get there? A gauge of the current reading and a timeline of every swing since 1940, for the United States and 27 other countries. Interpretive placements, offered to be disputed.
